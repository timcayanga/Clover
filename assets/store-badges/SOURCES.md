# Store badges

Official English download artwork, retrieved 2026-10-17. Preserve the artwork and proportions.

- App Store: https://developer.apple.com/assets/elements/badges/download-on-the-app-store.svg
- Apple usage guidance: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png

Google Play artwork includes its own transparent clear space. Match visible badge heights with CSS, without cropping or redrawing the artwork. Apple appears first. Badges link to Clover’s public listings and remain static.
